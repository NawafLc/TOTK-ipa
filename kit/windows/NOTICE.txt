TOTK IPA Builder

This program builds an iPhone/iPad app (IPA) of The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom from YOUR OWN game files:
the base game and the 1.4.3 update dumped from your Nintendo Switch, and the prod.keys from the same console. It
contains no game content and downloads none. Everything is done on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

You must own the game. The IPA you build contains the game made from your files: it is for your own use only. Do not
share it.

Nintendo.

The game runs through MacNativeGames (https://github.com/ly05010419/MacNativeGames, MIT license); see
kit\MacNativeGames\LICENSE and kit\MacNativeGames\THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md after installing. Included tools: Python
(PSF license), hactool (ISC license), SPIRV-Cross (Apache 2.0), the Ryujinx shader translator (MIT), and the Python
packages cryptography, lz4, capstone and nsz with their dependencies (their licenses are in their wheels).
